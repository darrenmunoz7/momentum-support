Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 4, 2026

Momentum is a weight tracking app built by Darren Munoz. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Storage

All data you enter into Momentum — including weight entries, goals, and settings — is stored locally on your device. Nothing is transmitted to any external server.

Data Collection

Momentum does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools in the app.

Third Parties

No data is shared with third parties. The app does not contain any third-party SDKs that collect user information.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual weight entries within the app, or delete all data from the Settings screen. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data from your device.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
